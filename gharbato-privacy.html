01 / Information

Information GharBato handles

GharBato is a property marketplace operated by Manjil Basnet. Depending on the features you use, the app may handle:

  • Account and profile detailsYour name, email address, phone number, selected country, profile photo, Firebase account identifier, and sign-in information. Firebase Authentication processes passwords and sign-in credentials; GharBato does not store your account password in its profile database. Sign-in may use email and password, Google, or phone verification.
  • Property listings and photosListing text, price, property type and features, address or area, map coordinates, availability, owner details, and images you upload for a listing.
  • Search and app activitySearch terms and filters, search history, saved properties, property views, reports, and notification preferences or records used to provide app features.
  • Messages and callsChat text, images sent in chats, sender and recipient identifiers, timestamps, and call setup information. When you make or receive a voice or video call, the audio or video stream is processed to connect the call.
  • Identity verification submissionsIf you choose to request verification, GharBato handles your name, email, document type, front and back document images, submission time, review status, and any review notes.
  • Location and device informationIf you grant location permission, the app can use your device location while you use map and location-picking features. A location you select for a property is saved with that listing. Firebase Cloud Messaging uses a device token to deliver push notifications. Google and other providers may also process basic device, app, network, and service diagnostic information when their services are used.
i

The AI property assistant sends your question, recent conversation context, and relevant property details to Google Gemini to generate a reply. AI conversation history is saved locally in the app on your device.

02 / Use

How information is used

GharBato uses information to create and secure accounts; publish and search property listings; show maps and nearby places; support saved properties and recommendations; enable user messaging and calls; send notifications; review optional identity-verification submissions; respond to reports and support requests; prevent misuse; and maintain the app.

Location is used for map and location features when you allow it. GharBato does not request background location permission. You can change location, camera, microphone, photo, and notification permissions in Android settings; some features may stop working if a permission is denied.

03 / Visibility

Sharing and visibility

Property listings and the information included in them are shown to other GharBato users so they can discover and contact listing owners. Chat messages and images are available to the participants in that conversation. Reports, support requests, and verification submissions are available to GharBato personnel who handle safety, support, or verification.

We share information with the service providers listed below to run the features you use. We do not sell personal information or use it for third-party ad targeting. We may also disclose information when required by law or when needed to protect users, the service, or legal rights.

04 / Device access

App permissions

  • LocationUsed when you choose current location or use map features. Selected property coordinates can be saved with the listing. Nearby-place searches send the map coordinates to the Overpass API endpoint used by the app.
  • Photos and cameraUsed when you choose or capture profile, property, verification, or chat images. The app uploads images you submit for those features.
  • Microphone and camera for callsUsed while you participate in voice or video calls. GharBato does not provide an in-app call-recording feature.
  • NotificationsOptional push notifications use a Firebase Cloud Messaging token associated with your account and device.

05 / Providers

Service providers

GharBato uses service providers to deliver app features. Depending on what you do in the app, information may be processed by:

  • Google Firebase and Google servicesFirebase Authentication, Realtime Database, Cloud Functions, and Cloud Messaging support accounts, app data, backend features, and notifications. Google location services help determine your device location when you grant permission. Google Gemini processes AI assistant prompts and related property context. See Google’s Privacy Policy.
  • CloudinaryStores and delivers profile, property, and chat images through image URLs, which may be viewed by anyone who obtains the URL. Identity-verification documents are encrypted with AES-GCM on your device before upload, so Cloudinary stores encrypted document files. The decryption keys are stored in Firebase records readable only by the submitting account and the authorized administrator. The administrator decrypts documents in the browser to review your verification request. A document file URL alone does not reveal its contents. See Cloudinary’s Privacy Policy.
  • ZEGOCLOUDProvides real-time voice and video calling. See ZEGOCLOUD’s Privacy Policy.
  • MapLibre and OpenStreetMapMapLibre renders maps on your device using map tiles requested from tile.openstreetmap.org. These requests reveal the map area being viewed, your IP address, and the app’s identifying user-agent to the tile provider. Map tiles are cached on your device. Nearby-place searches send the selected coordinates, search radius, and place categories to the Overpass endpoint at overpass-api.de. See the OpenStreetMap Foundation Privacy Policy.

These providers may process data in locations outside Nepal under their own service terms and privacy practices. Review their policies for details about their handling and retention.

06 / Your choices

Retention and deletion

We keep information for as long as needed to provide GharBato, maintain listings and conversations, handle verification and safety issues, comply with legal obligations, and resolve disputes. You can edit profile details, remove saved items, and delete some listings or messages from within the app.

You can start account deletion from Profile → Edit Profile → Delete Account. That action removes the Firebase sign-in account. To request removal of associated GharBato records and uploaded files as well, use the GharBato account deletion page or email dev.manjil.c@gmail.com from the email address or phone number linked to your account. We may ask you to verify that you own the account. Do not send your password, one-time code, or identity documents by email.

After verifying a request, we review and remove information we control unless it must be retained for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or dispute-resolution reasons. Limited copies may remain temporarily in provider backups or logs under their retention schedules.

07 / Safeguards

Security

Firebase Authentication handles account credentials, and app requests to Firebase, Cloudinary, and other service endpoints use encrypted network connections. We use service providers to host and deliver app data. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure, so keep your sign-in credentials private and contact us if you believe your account has been misused.

08 / Contact

Contact and policy updates

GharBato is operated by Manjil Basnet. For privacy questions, data requests, or concerns, email dev.manjil.c@gmail.com or use the portfolio contact form.
