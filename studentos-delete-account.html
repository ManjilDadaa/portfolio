Request StudentOS account deletion

Use this page if you no longer have access to the StudentOS app or prefer to make your request on the web. A verified request covers your StudentOS account, certificate details, and certificate files associated with that account.

  1. Enter the email address used for your StudentOS account.
  2. Submit the request. Manjil Basnet will verify the request using that account email.
  3. After verification, we aim to complete deletion within 30 days and will contact you if more information is needed.

Your account email and confirmation are stored in the StudentOS Supabase project for the developer to review. The form does not delete your account automatically or send an email notification. Please do not enter your password or attach certificate documents. Read the StudentOS Privacy Policy for details.
