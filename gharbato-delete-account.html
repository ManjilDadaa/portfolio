You can request deletion of your GharBato account and the personal information associated with it.

Start a request

How to request deletion

  1. Email GharBato supportUse the button below and include “GharBato account deletion request” in the subject.
  2. Identify your accountInclude the email address or phone number linked to your GharBato account and your profile name. Do not send your password, one-time code, or identity documents.
  3. We verify and process itWe may contact you to confirm that you own the account. After verification, we review and remove account information, listings, messages, verification records, and uploaded files we control, subject to the limited retention described below.
Email GharBato to request deletion

You can also start deletion in the app at Profile → Edit Profile → Delete Account. To request removal of associated listings, messages, verification records, and uploaded files, email support using the button above.

What happens next

Data retained for limited reasons

We may retain information when needed to meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent fraud or abuse, or protect the security of GharBato and its users. Copies may remain temporarily in service-provider backups or logs under their retention schedules. We will not use a deletion request as a reason to keep information longer than necessary.

For details about information handled by GharBato and its service providers, read the GharBato Privacy Policy.
